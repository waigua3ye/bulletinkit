How to Make a Church Bulletin in Word (Step by Step)

A six-page bulletin, built from a blank document, in about forty minutes — with the exact settings that keep it printable.

This is the whole process, in the order you should do it. Doing the page setup last is the single most common reason a bulletin ends up clipped or half-empty.

Step 1 — Set the paper and margins before you type

Layout → Size: choose Letter (8.5 × 11 in) or A4 (210 × 297 mm). Layout → Margins → Custom: 0.25 in on all four sides. Do not use "Narrow" — it is 0.5 in top and bottom on some versions and 0.5 in on all sides on others.

Then set your printer to 100% / Actual size. If the print dialog says "Fit to page", the margins you just set are being overridden.

Step 2 — Pick two fonts and stop

One serif for body text (Georgia, Garamond, or Times New Roman) and one sans for headings and labels (Calibri, Arial, or Open Sans). Two fonts is a design. Four fonts is an accident.

Body text at 10–11 pt, line spacing 1.15. Headings at 14–16 pt. Anything smaller than 9 pt will be unreadable for the half of your congregation over sixty.

Step 3 — Page 1 is the order of service, nothing else

Date, service time, the order of worship with who leads each item, the preacher, and the scripture readings in the order they occur. Put the announcements on page 2. The front page is what people hold while they walk in — it should answer "what am I doing for the next hour", not "what is happening on Thursday".

Starter copy — change the brackets

[Church Name] · [Month] [Day] · 10:00 a.m.

Call to Worship — [Leader]
Hymn: [Title and number]
Prayer of Confession — [Leader]
Reading: [Book Chapter:Verses] — [Reader]
Sermon: [Title] — [Preacher]
Offering · Prayers of the People — [Leader]
Closing Hymn: [Title and number]
Benediction — [Preacher]

Step 4 — Build pages 2–6 once, reuse every week

Page 2 announcements (three items maximum, each with a date and a person to ask). Page 3 prayers and people. Page 4 the month at a glance. Page 5 ways to serve, with a name and a phone number. Page 6 the back page — giving, contact details, and next week's date.

The week-to-week work is only pages 1 and 2. If you are rebuilding all six every week, you are doing five pages of unpaid work.

Step 5 — Save two versions

Save the .docx so you can edit it, then File → Save As → PDF for the version you print and email. Print the PDF, not the .docx — Word reflows differently on a different computer.

Frequently asked

How long should a bulletin be? Four to six pages for a congregation under 150. Beyond that people stop reading and start skimming the announcements.

Should I print double-sided? Yes if your printer supports it — it halves the paper and keeps the bulletin to three sheets. Set flip on long edge.

Can I do this in Google Docs? Yes, with one caveat: Docs has no reliable "flip on long edge" for booklets, so printing double-sided is a printer setting, not a document setting.
